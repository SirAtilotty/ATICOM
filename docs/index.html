All Time Communication

A chat app for you and your friends. Nobody else.

ATICOM is a small messenger for a closed group of friends. You can only get in with an invite. It runs in the browser, installs on your phone like a normal app, and lives on a server you own, even a Raspberry Pi.

Message

Everything a group chat needs, and nothing it doesn't.

No ads, no feeds, no strangers. Just the people you invited.

01

Private and group chats

Talk one on one or make a group. Add people, rename it, or leave whenever you want.

02

Photos and voice messages

Send a picture, or tap the mic to record. Voice messages play right inside the chat.

03

Swipe to reply

On a phone, swipe a message to the right to reply to it, so nobody loses track of what you meant.

04

Read receipts

One tick when it's sent, two when it's read. You'll also see when someone is typing or recording.

05

Notifications that stay quiet

Your phone gets notified even when the app is closed, but the notification never contains the message text.

06

English and Turkish

Switch the language from the login screen or your profile. Light and dark themes follow your phone.

Runs on your own server.

Every ATICOM is its own little world: its own users, messages and invites, with no connection to anyone else's.

  • Nothing to install but Node.jsNode 22.13 or newer. No other dependencies.
  • Made for a Raspberry PiWith Tailscale Funnel you get an HTTPS address without buying a domain or opening ports.
  • Your data stays in one folderThe database, photos and voice messages all live in data/, on your machine.

You're the admin.

Everything is handled from the terminal on the server. No admin panel to break into.

node server.js invite

Creates a one-time invite link that works for 7 days.

node server.js reset <username>

Gives someone a temporary password and logs out their open sessions.

node server.js remove <username>

Removes a member. Their old messages stay in the chat.

node server.js backup <folder>

Backs up the database and files, even while the server is running.
